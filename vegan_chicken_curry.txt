Ingredients:

450 g smoked tofu, cubed, coated with starch
1 large onion, chopped
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp ginger, minced
400 ml coconut milk
400 g diced tomatoes
2 tbsp curry powder
1 tsp turmeric
1 tsp cumin
1 tsp coriander
Salt and pepper to taste
2 tbsp vegetable oil

Preparation:

Fry coated tofu in pan with oil. Remove from pan when tofu is golden brown.
Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger in oil.
Add spices and tofu.
Add tomatoes and coconut milk, simmer for 5 minutes.
Season with salt and pepper.
