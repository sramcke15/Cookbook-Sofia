Chocolate chip cookies or just dough 
 
Ingredients: 
2 flax eggs (2tbs flax meal mixed with 5tbs water)
1/3-cup tapioca flour (not starch)
1/3-cup potato flour (not starch)
1/3-cup coconut flour
Approximately 1tbs coconut oil slightly soft, plus more for greasing
1tsp almond extract or vanilla extract or WHATEVER
1tsp baking soda
1/3-cup raw honey
1/3-cup cashew butter
Approximately ½-cup chocolate chips (if desired)
 
Procedure:
Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit.
Grease a cookie sheet with the extra coconut oil and set aside.
Make the flax eggs in a separate bowl by combining the water and flax and mixing slightly so all the flax meal is saturated, let sit for at least five minutes.
Mix all the ingredients together (except the chocolate chips) in a large bowl and then add the flax eggs and mix until everything is well incorporated ( using your hands works well).
Now, mix in the chips if desired.
The dough now needs to rest in the fridge for 30 minutes. I know it seems tedious, but it really makes a difference for gluten free items!
 
If your planning on just eating the dough its finished!
If you had your heart set on cookies…
Roll the dough into balls of a size somewhere in-between a quarter and a golf ball. It should make about 12 balls for 12 cookies. Bake them for about 12 minutes or until the bottoms are brown.
