2. Potato soup/clam chowder 
Or just potato soup
 
Ingredients: 
One russet potato
Half of a large white onion
3tbs butter or coconut oil
½ can of coconut milk
about 2,5 cups of broth (chicken or veggie)
salt (lots)
paprika ( just a bit)
desired cheese or nutritional yeast ( I use sheep’s manchego)
and about 1tbs of honey
for the clam chowder version…
one six oz. can of chopped clams
 
Procedure:
Begin by caramelizing the onions with the honey and butter or oil in a large pot.
Once they are nice and brown (about 10 minuntes) add the broth and potato and let it boil for 30 minutes. The potato should be easily stabbed with a butter knife!
 
To make the potato soup…
All the pot to a food processor with all remaining ingredients and blitz! Until all is smooth. You can put all the pure back into the pot to warm it up again.
 
To make the chowder…
Process like you would for the soup, but leave out about one third of the potato! Cut that part of the potato up into chunks to be added later. As you puree the other ingredients pour some of the clam juice into the soup. The more the clammier! Them put that back into the pot to warm back up a bit and toss all of the clams in, and the chunked potato bits.
